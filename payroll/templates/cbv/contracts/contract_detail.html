{% load i18n %} {% comment %} The contract detail modal. It was fifteen labelled rows in a grid — start date beside job role beside filing status — with no answer to the question someone actually opens a contract to ask: what will this person be paid, and which components decide it. The components were not shown at all, because nothing linked them to a contract on screen; they reach an employee through Allowance.specific_employees, and a salary structure is only what puts them there. So: a summary bar of the figures that drive a payslip, then two tabs. Terms holds the fifteen fields. Components shows every component that reaches this employee AND how it reaches them — through the structure, targeted directly, applying to everybody, or condition-based. Those four look identical on a payslip and are completely different when you want to stop one. {% endcomment %}
