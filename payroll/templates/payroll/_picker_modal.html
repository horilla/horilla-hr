{% comment %} Shared styles for the two "start from something ready-made" choosers: salary components, and tax configurations. They are the same interaction — pick several from a grouped list, load them, nothing is overwritten — and they had drifted into two different designs: one a cramped five-column table where the descriptions fought the Slabs and Top rate columns for width, the other a plain list with no close button at all. One stylesheet, one row shape, so the two read the same and neither can drift again on its own. The row: a checkbox, a name with a kind badge, a one-line summary, an optional caveat, and optional figures on the right. Country or region is the group heading rather than a column, which is what frees the width for the summary. {% endcomment %}
