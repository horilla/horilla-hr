{% load i18n %} {% comment %} A payroll form in the app's standard modal chrome. The fragment underneath (form_fragment.html) is written for the contracts page: a centred card with a Back button, whose form posts into #listContainer. Dropped into a modal as-is it had no title and no close button, and its save would have swapped a contract list into whatever #listContainer happened to be on the page behind it. So the fragment is given the header, close button and body that every other Horilla modal has, and told to post back into the modal instead. It is the same fragment either way, so a contract form looks and behaves the same wherever it is opened — which is the point of using it rather than writing a second one. {% endcomment %}
